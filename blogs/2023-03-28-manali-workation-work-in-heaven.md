#digitalnomads #anar Our Engineering team had an amazing workation in Manali, a picturesque hill station in Himachal Pradesh, India. We spent a week in February 2023 working and exploring this beautiful destination, surrounded by the Beas river and the snow-capped peaks of the Pir Panjal mountain range. We stayed at a cozy hostel that offered great WiFi, delicious food, and stunning views.
