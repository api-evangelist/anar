2022 — A year in review for Anar When Sanjay and I started Anar in 2019, we envisioned a platform that created economic opportunities for every small business in India. A platform which gave them 10x exposure to the market — finding buyers and suppliers, viewing and uploading their product catalogues from the comfort of their shops and offices, no matter where they are. We wanted to crack the Indian market before making strides globally, so we started our journey in the clothing industry in India.
